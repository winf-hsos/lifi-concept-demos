the raw picture is 256 × 256 points, three bytes each, 196,608 bytes. every byte is xor-ed with the key byte beneath it; a short key repeats. the neighbouring team simply displays what it read.
the first button needs the one-byte key. it keeps the smoothest of the 256 results: a picture has neighbours that resemble each other, noise has not.
the method may be public. the key must not, and it must be long enough that neither guessing nor looking gives it away.
